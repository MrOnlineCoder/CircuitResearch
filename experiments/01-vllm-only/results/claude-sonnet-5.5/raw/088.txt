Reading the schematic: the top rail is VDD, and there are two PMOS devices on top (left and right) and two lower transistors, MP1 and MP2, which are drawn as NMOS.

**Top PMOS pair**
- Both sources connect to VDD.
- Their gates are tied together and to the left PMOS drain, which makes the left PMOS diode-connected.
- The left PMOS drain is net1. It also runs down to the drain of MP1.
- The right PMOS drain is net2. It connects to the drain and gate of MP2 (diode-connected), and to the gate of MP1.

**Lower NMOS pair**
- MP1 has its drain on net1, its gate on net2, and its source on net3, which goes to RP1.
- MP2 has its drain on net2, its gate on net2, and its source on GND.
- RP1 runs from net3 to GND.

**Gate net:** the line from the MP1 gate to the MP2 gate has a dot that connects it to the MP2 drain wire. The MP2 gate is therefore net2, the same net as its drain.
