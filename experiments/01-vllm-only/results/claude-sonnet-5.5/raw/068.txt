This is a bandgap core. Both bipolar transistors are PNP with the collector and base tied to ground, and the emitter on top.

The left branch runs from the op-amp's inverting node (net1) through R1 to net3, the emitter of the N-times-area PNP. The right branch has the non-inverting node (net2) connected directly to the emitter of the single PNP.

Each R2 connects the op-amp output (VOUT) to one of the two input nets. The left R2 goes from the output to net1, and the right R2 goes from the output to net2.

The diagram shows the base and collector shorted together and to ground, so I treat both as GND.
