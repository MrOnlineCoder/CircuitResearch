Looking at the schematic: the top two transistors are PMOS with their sources tied to VDD (the top symbol is a supply bar, not ground). The pipeline labelled that net GND, so the sources of M1 and M2 should be on a VDD net.

The bottom two transistors, M3 and M4, are drawn with arrows on the source side, pointing into the device from the source. In the image, the arrow on the bottom-left transistor is at the source (bottom) and points inward toward the channel, which is the usual NMOS symbol. The pipeline's NMOS type for M3 and M4 is therefore correct.
